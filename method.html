Sources, checked live

The ladder level says how a planner gets the data: 3 a service that answers by area, 2 a file at a stable address, 1 published on a schedule as a page or PDF, 0 a phone call. Moving a source up one rung is the cheapest improvement DHS can make.

Coverage against the DHS goals

Goal 1 asks for hazard-specific AFN maps for every county and 100% of census tracts. These are the residents inside each published hazard zone, computed from 2020 Census blocks.

Checked against past events

Goal 2 asks for estimates validated against past events. The same formula, untuned, on the area each event affected, beside the published shelter count.

Planning factors

“Published” factors come from a named standard; “assumption” factors are ours and are the first things to agree with DHS and the counties.
