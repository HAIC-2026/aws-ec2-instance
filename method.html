Data in this browser

AllReady has no accounts and no database. Everything it keeps is stored in this browser on this computer, and nothing here leaves the machine unless you export it. This is the full list.

Data classes: every source feed is public; every figure derived from them is public and area-level; planner entries (markup, partner commitments, supply counts) are internal planning information about organisations and facilities, never residents.
