ESF-6 mass care · access and functional needs

Already in the plan.

When a hurricane, wildfire, tsunami or flood threatens Hawaiʻi, the people who need the most help should be in the plan before it arrives. AllReady brings together where residents with access and functional needs live, what each county’s shelters can support, and who acts when.

Reading live feeds…

  • Who and whereResidents by census tract across the CMIST needs: communication, medical, independence, safety and support, transportation.
  • What it takesShelter space, accessible space, power, water, transport and more, set against what the counties have on record.
  • Who actsCounties lead mass care and the State supports. Decision points, contacts and an assistant that reads the live map.
